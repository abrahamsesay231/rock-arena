Game guide

A quick guide to playing Rock, Paper, Scissors against the computer.

How to play

  1. Choose rock, paper, or scissors using the buttons on the game board.
  2. The computer makes a random choice at the same time.
  3. The winning choice earns one point. A tie earns no points.

The rules

  • Rock beats scissors.
  • Scissors beats paper.
  • Paper beats rock.

Your scores are saved in this browser, so they remain when you return to the game.
